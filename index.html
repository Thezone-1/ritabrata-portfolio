How I
work

Three habits, in order. The third is the one most performance work skips.

i
Diagnose
Load, stress, spike and endurance testing until the system tells the truth about its limits. JMeter scripts parameterised and version controlled, never clicked through by hand.
ii
Instrument
Prometheus, Grafana and Datadog wired so the next failure surfaces in minutes instead of arriving as a support ticket. On one platform that cut detection time by a fifth.
iii
Hand it over
A test only I can run is a test that dies when I leave. Suites go into Jenkins, dashboards go to the team, and the findings get written up in language the business side can act on.
